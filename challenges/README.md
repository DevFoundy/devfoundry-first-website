# Practice with the course

**Try the challenge before opening the answer guide.**

1. Complete the relevant workbook module.
2. Open [challenges and pass criteria](challenges.md).
3. Make and test a change in your working portfolio.
4. When you have tried, compare your approach with [the answer guide](answers.md).

For the final project, use [the brief and rubric](final-project.md), [final checklist](final-checklist.md) and [test log](test-log.md). Keep your personal test results outside this course repository unless you intentionally want to share them. Never add private contact information to a public submission.
