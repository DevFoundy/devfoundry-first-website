# Code of Conduct

DevFoundry welcomes beginners and contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Ask questions freely, explain patiently and assume that people are still learning.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code rather than attacking people.
- Explain feedback with enough context for a learner to act on it.
- Respect privacy and boundaries, and accept reasonable requests to stop.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexual harassment, deliberate hostility and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests and other project-managed discussions. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or block repeated or serious abuse. They should consider context, explain decisions where appropriate and handle reports as discreetly as the available channel allows.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's report-abuse controls for abusive public content. If you need a private maintainer contact and none is listed, make a non-sensitive request for one without naming affected people or sharing incident details. No dedicated private conduct inbox is currently advertised. Do not post private information in a public issue.
